How to read the estimate

Typical uses median dining durations. Cautious uses 80th-percentile durations for each group; it is a planning scenario, not an 80% on-time probability. Status includes your chosen buffer. Both scenarios include seating, ordering, food preparation, eating and payment in the dining duration.

Routes are preset demo distances. The queue follows strict first-in, first-out seating with one group per table. New arrivals before you reach the restaurant are not forecast. Snapshot time is your selected departure time; this is not a live feed.
